📖 How CourseClear Works

1. Managing Courses & Themes

From the home dashboard, click + Add New Course or select an existing course card. Inside each course, you can set your credit hours, set a target grade, and pick an aesthetic color theme (Pink, Purple, Teal, Yellow, or Indigo).

2. Syllabus Importer & Weights

Instead of manually typing every category, paste your syllabus breakdown text (e.g., "Exams 40%, Homework 30%") into the Quick Syllabus Importer and click auto-fill. Make sure your category weights total exactly 100%!

3. Study Buddy Feedback Coach

In each course view, your AI Study Buddy compares your live percentage to your target grade, highlights your lowest scoring assignment, and recommends tailored study tips.

4. What-If Grade Simulator

Inside any course detail view, use the What-If Simulator to test hypothetical future scores and see how they impact your overall percentage instantly.

5. Backup & Restore

Go to the GPA & Goals Hub anytime to export your data as a JSON file or import a past backup so you never lose your progress.
