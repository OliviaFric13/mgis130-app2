📖 How CourseClear Works

1. Managing Courses & Themes

From the home dashboard, click + Add New Course or select an existing course card. Inside each course, you can set your credit hours, set a target grade, and pick an aesthetic color theme (Pink, Purple, Teal, Yellow, or Indigo).

2. Category Weights

In the setup step for each course, manually enter your grading rubric categories and weights (e.g., Exams 40%, Homework 30%). Make sure your weights total exactly 100%!

3. Study Buddy Feedback Coach

In each course view, your AI Study Buddy compares your live percentage to your target grade, highlights your lowest scoring assignment, and recommends tailored study tips right after you log a score.

4. What-If Grade Simulator & Target Needed Calculator

Inside any course detail view, expand the simulator drawer to test hypothetical future scores AND calculate the exact score you need on remaining work to reach your target grade!
